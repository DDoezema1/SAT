## Joining two complete sentences

### RW-SEC-01 · Boundaries · difficulty 2

Monarch butterflies in eastern North America migrate up to 3,000 miles each fall to forests in central ______ no single butterfly completes the round trip, since the journey back north spans several generations.

*Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?*

- A) Mexico,
- B) Mexico;
- C) Mexico
- D) Mexico, however,

**Answer:** B

**Why:** Both sides are complete sentences (“Monarch butterflies … migrate …” and “no single butterfly completes the round trip”), so they need a semicolon: “Mexico;”. A comma alone, or “, however,” is a comma splice; no punctuation is a run-on.

## Colons

### RW-SEC-06 · Boundaries · difficulty 2
